Dungeon Master RAG Assistant

Fast, sourced answers from the D&D SRD 5.1

A lightweight rules assistant built for Dungeon Masters who need a reliable answer without stopping the game. It retrieves relevant passages from the official SRD 5.1, generates an answer with citations and refuses questions outside the source.

  • Python
  • RAG
  • Gemini / Groq
  • ChromaDB
  • LlamaIndex
Source
D&D SRD 5.1 · official CC BY 4.0 rules reference
Retrieval
Hierarchical chapter and section chunks with hybrid search
Generation
Gemini first, with Groq fallback
Evaluation
14/14 rule questions passed in the final saved run

How it works

RAG pipeline

01

Structure the rules

The SRD is split by chapter and section so spellcasting, combat and conditions keep their context.

02

Retrieve the evidence

Hybrid retrieval finds the relevant chunks and keeps their chapter, section and page metadata.

03

Answer or abstain

The model answers from the retrieved context, cites its sources and blocks questions outside the SRD.

Built and evaluated in Google Colab.
